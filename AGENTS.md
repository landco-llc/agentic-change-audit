# Agent Guidance

This is a public repository. Do not place secrets, personal data, private paths,
private links, credentials, customer information, or private ledger content in
repository files, commits, issues, or pull requests.

The Parent is controller-only. Before every control action, it freshly reads
the applicable campaign and Work records, fixed base, expected head, allowed
scope, routing metadata, required evidence, checks, findings, comments,
delegation validity, and protected target state. It may create minimal
public-safe lifecycle shells and bindings, route one authorized Child role at
a time, transport Child-authored material byte-exactly, verify deterministic
evidence and state predicates, account for correction cycles, and stop
fail-closed. It must not substantively author or repair Work instructions,
implementation evidence, audit instructions or reports, findings, correction
instructions, or implementation/correction/audit/re-audit content. Incomplete
or unusable substantive output is re-routed to a Child or stopped, not repaired
by the Parent.

Substantive instruction and evidence authoring, implementation, independent
audit, correction, and fresh re-audit belong to separated Child contexts. An
audit is fixed-head and read-only: a correction changes the head and therefore
requires a new independent re-audit. No Child may mark Ready, merge, release,
submit, publish, or deploy.

A finite campaign may continue only under an explicit campaign binding with a
finite ordered Work set or positive upper bound, authorized scope envelope,
validity condition, standing-delegation reference, and named terminal Human
Gate. Only one Work is ACTIVE at a time. Every Work keeps its own instruction,
scope, risk, exact-head audit, checks, Ready, merge, and post-merge evidence;
one Work's PASS never carries into another Work.

Treat durable repository history as public-safe evidence only. Keep active
private operating detail in a separate private ledger; never copy it here.
Fast Track is standing Human delegation, not AI risk acceptance or blanket
merge permission. Its authority instantiates separately for each listed Work
and exact head. Hard gates stop work until their named Human or external
prerequisite is satisfied. Do not infer Ready, merge, release, or deployment
authority from an audit result.

Same-Work correction dispatches are capped at three, and a materially repeated
finding after fresh re-audit stops same-line correction immediately. A commit,
agent, context, session, PR, or campaign position does not reset that history.

Temporary Parent model escalation is limited to a named complex controller
judgment, must use only an accepted/current model/profile pairing already
represented by active local routing, must record its bounded reason and
de-escalation point, and ends immediately when that judgment ends or is handed
to a Child. Candidate, proposed, shadow, successor, experimental, and non-active
Astra material is not executable authority and cannot activate a model or
widen Parent powers.

See `docs/ai-dev-ops/orchestration/` for the machine-readable work-record and
work-result contracts, local routing reference, and control-state model.
